# 40Labs / Angstrom visual foundation

The supplied 40Labs emblem and wordmark are the source of truth for Angstrom's visual identity. Preserve the original artwork and proportions; keep Angstrom as the product name under 40Labs.

## Core palette

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Carbon | `#0c1117` | Main background |
| Chalk | `#efefec` | Primary text |
| Amber | `#e9a51a` | Key accents and active controls |
| Slate | `#92999e` | Secondary text |
| Graphite | `#2b3035` | Fine dividers |

These are interface colors selected from the supplied references, not verified original brand specifications.

## Shape and type

Use fine concentric circles, restrained orbital details, open line terminals, and simple geometric forms. Match the logos' technical character with widely spaced small labels and clear, lightweight numerals. Preserve generous space and readable controls. Reserve amber for small accents; avoid large bright fills in bedside views. Avoid continuous animation or decorative clutter on a nightstand display.

## Assets

`apps/display/public/assets/40labs-emblem.png` and `apps/display/public/assets/40labs-wordmark.png` are unmodified copies of the user-supplied screenshots. They establish the direction and appear in the display header/footer. Replace them with original high-resolution or vector artwork when available, rather than tracing or inventing a new logo.

Keep this foundation consistent across future phone interfaces, display nodes, settings pages, and assistant visual states.
